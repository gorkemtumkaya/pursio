# Pursio

**Turn any hobby into a path you can actually follow.**

Type a hobby (bouldering, film photography, guitar...) and Pursio builds a personal skill tree for it. Each skill comes with small quests and fresh videos and articles from across the web, so you always know what to learn next and can see how far you've come.

## Why
Getting better at a hobby is hard when content is scattered, you don't know what to learn next, and progress is invisible. Pursio gives you a clear path, a reason to come back every day, and a small companion, Wick, who lights the way.

## Features
- AI-generated skill tree for any hobby
- Small quests for every skill
- Fresh content matched to the skill you're on
- XP, levels and streaks
- Wick, a lantern spirit who dresses for your hobby
